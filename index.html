The 8 UAE-specific rules we check
  1. TIN format — Participant ID must be 10 digits, the first 10 of a valid TRN.
  2. Scheme code — Seller electronic identifier must be exactly 0235.
  3. Transaction type code — 8-character flag string, only 0 or 1.
  4. AED line amounts — fields 48 & 49 required when invoice currency ≠ AED.
  5. Legal registration ID type — must be TL, EID, PAS, or CD.
  6. Specification Identifier — must match a published PINT AE value.
  7. Currency consistency — Total tax in accounting currency, line totals in invoice currency.
  8. Tax category math — taxable amount × rate must equal tax amount per category.

How it works

Each file is parsed in your browser, identified as a Tax Invoice (51 fields) or a Commercial Invoice (49 fields), then run through two layers of checks: structural mandatory-field presence, and the eight UAE-specific rules from the MoF Guidelines V1.0.
